THE QUESTION

“Should I buy Bitcoin now?”

↓

MAYBE THE BETTER QUESTION

“What happens if I buy now and I am wrong?”

BUY NOW OR WAIT?

The frustrating part is that both decisions can feel wrong.

01

You buy now.

Bitcoin falls 20% next month.

“I should have waited.”
02

You wait.

Bitcoin rises 20% next month.

“I should have bought.”

If your decision is judged only by what Bitcoin does next, you can regret either choice.

DECISION BEFORE PREDICTION

What if the price is not the first thing you should check?

You cannot control whether Bitcoin rises or falls tomorrow.

You can control how much you buy, how much of your portfolio becomes crypto, how much loss you can tolerate, how long you plan to hold and what would make you exit.

Instead of asking “Will Bitcoin go up?”
→
Ask “Does this decision still make sense if Bitcoin goes down?”

You do not need to predict Bitcoin to detect a bad fit with your own rules.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Four things you can actually check.

01

Portfolio fit

How large will your total crypto exposure become after this purchase?

02

Loss tolerance

What would a 20%, 30% or larger decline mean in actual money for you?

03

Time horizon

Are you prepared to hold if the market moves against you for months or years?

04

Exit rule

What would make you sell, reduce or rebalance before emotions take over?
