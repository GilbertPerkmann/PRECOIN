BUY NOW OR WAIT?

The difficult part is that nobody knows the next move.

Bitcoin could fall after you buy. It could also rise while you wait. Trying to predict the perfect entry can turn the decision into an endless guessing game.

A more useful question is: if you buy today and Bitcoin moves sharply against you, would the decision still make sense under the rules you already chose for your own portfolio?

SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Suppose you are considering buying €2,000 of Bitcoin today.

Portfolio €20,000
Already invested in crypto €3,000
Planned Bitcoin purchase €2,000
Maximum crypto allocation 20%
Planned horizon 3–5 years

What should you check before deciding?

After the purchase, your crypto exposure would rise from 15% to approximately 22.7%. That would exceed the 20% portfolio limit you set for yourself. Whether Bitcoin rises tomorrow does not change that conflict.

DECISION BEFORE PREDICTION

The question is not only “Will Bitcoin go up?” It is “Can I live with this decision if it goes down?”

Buying now becomes easier to evaluate when you stop judging the decision only by the next price move and start checking it against your own financial limits.

Portfolio fit

How large would your crypto exposure become after the planned purchase?

Time horizon

Are you prepared to hold through a long period of volatility?

Loss tolerance

How large a temporary decline could you realistically tolerate without abandoning the plan?

Exit rule

What would make you sell, reduce or rebalance before the market moves?
