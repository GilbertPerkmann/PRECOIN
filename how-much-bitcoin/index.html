POSITION SIZE

There isn't one correct amount of Bitcoin for everyone.

€500 might be a small Bitcoin position for one investor and an extremely large position for another.

The amount alone tells you very little. What matters is how large the Bitcoin purchase becomes relative to your overall investable portfolio and the limits you set for yourself.

SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Suppose you want to buy €2,000 of Bitcoin.

Portfolio €20,000
Already invested in crypto €3,000
Planned Bitcoin purchase €2,000
Maximum crypto allocation 20%

What happens after the purchase?

Your crypto exposure would increase from 15% of the portfolio to approximately 22.7%. That would exceed the 20% limit you defined for yourself.

WHY POSITION SIZE MATTERS

The question isn't only “How much Bitcoin can I buy?” It's also “How much risk am I adding?”

A planned purchase can look reasonable when viewed as a euro amount while becoming much larger when viewed as a percentage of your entire portfolio.
