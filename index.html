The overlooked risk

The market isn't your only risk. You are too.

When prices move quickly, investors often change the rules they created when they were calm.

FOMO

Buying because everyone else suddenly seems to be making money.

Oversizing

Increasing a position far beyond the risk limit you originally set.

Panic

Abandoning a long-term plan because the market moved sharply.

Rule changing

Rewriting the strategy after money and emotion are already involved.

How PRECOIN works

A preflight check for your investment decision.

01

Set your rules

Define your maximum crypto allocation, position size, investment horizon, loss tolerance and exit logic.

02

Enter the decision

Tell PRECOIN what asset you want to buy, how much you want to invest and why.

03

Run the preflight

PRECOIN compares the planned action with your own predefined rules and highlights conflicts.

04

You decide

You still make the investment decision. PRECOIN simply helps you see what emotion may be hiding.
