BEFORE YOU BUY BITCOIN

“Should I buy Bitcoin now?” may be the wrong first question.

Nobody can know with certainty whether Bitcoin will rise or fall after you buy it. A more useful first step is to ask whether the decision fits rules you defined before fear, greed or FOMO entered the picture.

Before buying Bitcoin, consider how much of your portfolio the investment would represent, how long you intend to hold, why you are buying and what would cause you to sell, reduce or rebalance the position.

BITCOIN INVESTMENT RISK

Bitcoin's price isn't your only risk. Your own behavior matters too.

Bitcoin can move sharply in both directions. During large price moves, investors can abandon rules they created when they were calm.

FOMO buying

Buying Bitcoin because the price is rising and everyone else suddenly appears to be making money.

Oversized positions

Investing more in Bitcoin than the portfolio allocation you originally considered acceptable.

Panic selling

Abandoning a long-term Bitcoin plan because the market suddenly falls.

Changing the rules

Rewriting your investment strategy after money, emotion and market movement are already involved.

BEFORE INVESTING

Questions to answer before buying Bitcoin

Why do I want to buy Bitcoin?

Write down the reason before you buy. A long-term investment thesis is different from buying because Bitcoin has suddenly risen or fallen in price. If your reason changes with the market every week, the decision may be driven more by emotion than by a plan.

How much Bitcoin should I buy?

There is no universal amount that is appropriate for everyone. A useful decision starts by comparing the planned purchase with your total investable portfolio and the maximum crypto allocation you are willing to accept.

What if Bitcoin drops sharply after I buy?

Consider that scenario before investing. If a large temporary decline would force you to sell, create financial stress or cause you to abandon your plan, that matters when choosing the size of the position.

How long am I willing to hold Bitcoin?

Your investment horizon changes the decision. Someone planning to hold for months is making a different decision from someone planning to hold for five or ten years. Define the horizon before the market moves.

What would make me sell Bitcoin?

Define what would invalidate your original reasoning. The important point is to decide what evidence, portfolio limit or change in your original thesis would cause you to sell, reduce or rebalance.

BITCOIN DECISION FAQ

Common questions before investing in Bitcoin

Is now a good time to buy Bitcoin?

Nobody can reliably know what Bitcoin's price will do next. PRECOIN therefore does not try to identify the perfect entry. It helps you examine whether a planned purchase fits your own portfolio limits, investment horizon and decision rules.

Is PRECOIN a Bitcoin price predictor?

No. PRECOIN does not predict Bitcoin prices, market tops, bottoms or future returns.

Does PRECOIN tell me whether to buy or sell Bitcoin?

No. PRECOIN is designed as a decision-checking tool. It highlights potential conflicts between a planned action and rules you defined for yourself.

Can PRECOIN prevent investment losses?

No. Bitcoin and other crypto assets can lose substantial value. PRECOIN cannot prevent losses or guarantee investment outcomes. Its purpose is to make the reasoning behind a decision more explicit before you act.

Is PRECOIN financial advice?

No. PRECOIN does not provide financial, investment or trading advice. You remain responsible for your own decisions.
